# Maintain the team wiki

Read [the shared operating contract](../../references/operating-contract.md), then follow **Maintain** in [the shared workflows](../../references/workflows.md).

Use the required Atlassian app. Scope scans to the verified root subtree and report coverage. Health checks are read-only; repair or publish only when requested. Preserve distinct human work, resume partial operations, and route disputed changes through review. Return prioritized findings with evidence and concrete repairs.
